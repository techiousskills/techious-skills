01

Operating a Computer

Your starting point if you've never sat at a computer with confidence before.

  • The parts of a computer (monitor, CPU, keyboard, mouse) and what each one does
  • Switching on, logging in and finding your way around the desktop
  • Using a mouse and keyboard comfortably, and opening and closing programs
  • Simple troubleshooting, like restarting safely when something freezes

Microsoft Word

Write and format documents that actually look professional.

  • Typing, editing and formatting text: fonts, sizes, alignment and spacing
  • Working with tables, images and page layout
  • Saving, printing and sharing a finished document
  • Building real documents: letters, CVs and simple reports

Microsoft Excel

Spreadsheets that organize your numbers and do the math for you.

  • Understanding rows, columns and cells, and entering data cleanly
  • Basic formulas and functions, like SUM and AVERAGE
  • Sorting, organizing and formatting a spreadsheet so it's easy to read
  • Creating simple charts and printing your work

Presentations that hold a room's attention from start to finish.

  • Building slides with text, images and clean layouts
  • Using themes, transitions and animations without overdoing it
  • Structuring a presentation so your ideas flow logically
  • Presenting with confidence in front of a class, a room or a client
